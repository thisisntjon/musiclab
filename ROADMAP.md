# Where contributions help most

The priority is creative access: let someone hear their influence, explore music and keep their own contribution. Genre names are landmarks, not a claim that MusicLab covers all music.

This is a priority guide, not a delivery schedule. Check open issues and discuss substantial changes before building.

1. **Dependable personal work.** Repeated Keep/save/reopen, imported songs, failed storage, held notes and accurate export.
2. **Understandable controls.** Clear requested-versus-playing feedback, keyboard and switch routes, readable focus, reduced motion and recovery.
3. **Evidence from real devices.** Describe the exact phone, browser or assistive setup used. Desktop emulation is useful but does not establish physical-device support.
4. **Coherent musical choices.** Compare alternatives over identical backing. Preserve recognizable phrases and personal material; more presets alone are not success.
5. **Useful layer exploration.** Extend the proven wheel interaction in small, reversible steps. Instruments select sound; they do not automatically provide continuous timbre morphing.
6. **Contributor experience.** Reproducible examples, accurate instructions, small fixtures and improvements that make the source easier to understand.

Longer-term ideas include richer optional musical demonstrations, further bounded phrase development, richer layer control, chosen returns/choruses and coherent unnamed combinations. Learned models are experiments to compare against simpler methods, not prerequisites or claims about current runtime capabilities.

MusicLab stays free. Its code and bundled assets have the licenses recorded in [LICENSE](LICENSE) and [NOTICES.md](NOTICES.md). We do not claim ownership of shared musical principles. Particular compositions, recordings and software still deserve their own attribution and licensing care.

Start with [contribution guidance](CONTRIBUTING.md), [the architecture map](ARCHITECTURE.md), or [play feedback](https://github.com/thisisntjon/musiclab/issues/new/choose).
