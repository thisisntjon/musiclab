Open MusicLab

For parents

MusicLab lets a child or a beginner listen to a band, change its direction and play along. They can keep what they make and return to it. It is made for children and beginners; it is not proven for every child. It is still in development.

In one line

Listen, steer the band and play along. Press Make my tune to explore tunes over that band. Compare with Original, undo a move, and keep the tune you choose. The main music tools are free; no account or payment is needed.

Start together

  1. Start with your device volume low. Press Listen when you are ready.
  2. Move through the music, or use the buttons to choose without dragging.
  3. Tap the on-screen keys or play with A, S, D, F, G, H, J and K. Z and X move the keys down or up.
  4. Press Make my tune. Turn the tune wheel to try another idea. In House, Reggae, Soul, Ska, Rocksteady and Swing, moving outward also shapes the same idea. Other styles use their existing tune patterns; the line beside Make my tune explains what is ready.
  5. Use Original to compare with your first tune, and Undo move to go back. Keep this melody opens the save screen. Optional musical help is a choice, not a required step.
  6. Use Stop whenever you want. Volume and Calm are available while you explore.

There is no score to beat. Listening, playing a single note, or changing a sound are all ways to explore. Help with a control does not take away the person's musical choices.

Make it comfortable

Calm reduces visual effects. The app also follows your device's reduced-motion setting. Keyboard and switch controls are available; look in Help and settings. Pictures and words accompany sound choices.

MusicLab limits its output, but it cannot set the loudness of your speakers or headphones. Choose a comfortable device volume. Different people need different sounds, displays and kinds of help.

On a first visit the full instrument sounds may take time to load. Once the app is ready and you press Listen and explore, music can start on simpler built-in sounds. The full sounds join at the next bar when ready. A line under the music says so while it happens. If the app itself cannot load, use Try again. If only the full sounds fail, keep playing with built-in sounds or use Load sounds again in Help.

What stays here

No account or advertising is needed. In the normal static listening journey, the app loads its own files and makes the music on your device. Session diagnostics and working songs are stored in this browser; session diagnostics are not posted to a server.

The optional humming feature asks for microphone permission. Its recording is processed on this device. You can skip it and still make music.

Keep a copy

Keep this melody selects the tune; then press Save here to save it in this browser. Download editable song keeps a separate copy you can reopen. If saving fails, download that copy before closing the page. Unkept playing is not safe from a crash.

A browser save belongs to that browser and device. Clearing browser data can remove it. Download audio makes a WAV recording; it cannot reopen as editable notes. Keep the editable song file too.

What we know

The app is not a validated learning or accessibility tool for every child. Technical tests check parts of its behavior; they do not establish that someone will enjoy it or learn music. Independent reviews have found and we have fixed editing and preview defects; reviews continue before each release.

Tested in: Chrome and Edge on Windows, in desktop and phone-sized windows, with mouse and with keyboard only. Other browsers and phones are listed here as they are checked. This is information for trying MusicLab, not a promise of suitability for a particular person.
